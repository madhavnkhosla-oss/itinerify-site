🏢 FAQ for Societies / Buildings

1. What are the two operating models?

Model A: Society-Funded

The society pays per registered resident (or as per their agreement)

Residents attend at zero or subsidized cost

Model B: Resident-Funded

Residents pay directly for each class they attend

No cost to the society

2. Who manages the coaches?

Either:

The site uses coaches supplied by our platform, or

Their own in-house/existing coaches

Both are easily managed through the app.

3. What does the app manage for us?

Class scheduling

Registration

Attendance tracking

Coach payments

Resident payments

Reports & insights

4. Can we switch operating models later?

Yes, societies can move between Model A and Model B anytime.
