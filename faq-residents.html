❓ FAQ for Residents

1. What is this app for?

It’s a central platform where you can browse fitness classes available in your society, register for a session, and make payments if required.

2. How do I register for a class?

Just open the app → select a class → tap Register. Your spot is instantly reserved.

3. Can I cancel after registering?

No. Once a class is confirmed, cancellations aren’t allowed as coaches and class resources are scheduled in advance.

4. Who are the coaches?

Your building may use:

Coaches provided by us, or

Existing coaches already associated with your society

Either way, all coaches on the app are approved by your society.

5. Will I get reminders?

Yes. Class timings and reminders are shared in advance through the app.
