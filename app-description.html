Company Summary

We are a modern group fitness company that provides professional fitness coaches, specialized group classes, and a seamless digital platform for residents to register, schedule, and participate in those classes.

Our focus is on bringing high-quality wellness experiences directly into residential societies by offering curated programs like Yoga/Pilates and Aqua Aerobics, along with a simple app that manages registrations, payments, and class schedules.

We partner with societies to enhance their amenities with expert-led sessions, reliable class management, and a smooth experience for both residents and coaches.

📘 App Summary (What the App Does)

This app is a simple, modern platform designed for residential societies and buildings that offer group fitness or wellness classes.

Residents can:

View available classes

Register for sessions

Pay for classes, when required

Track attendance, timings, and updates from coaches

Buildings and societies get a smooth way to manage classes, coaches, schedules, and payments under one platform.

Coaches—either provided by us or existing trainers tied to the building—can run their classes through the app, manage attendance, and get paid directly through the system.

The building/society chooses from two operating models, and also decides whether they pay per resident or residents pay individually.

Classes offered

🧘‍♀️ Yoga / Pilates (Hybrid Class)

A calm, powerful blend of Yoga and Pilates designed for all fitness levels. This class focuses on:

Core strengthening

Flexibility & mobility

Posture correction

Breath–body coordination

Stress relief & mindfulness

It's gentle yet deeply effective — perfect for residents looking to improve balance, tone muscles, and build long-term stability. Whether you’re a beginner or advanced, each session adapts to your pace.

🌊 Aqua Aerobics

A fun, low-impact, high-energy workout done in the pool. Aqua Aerobics is ideal for all age groups, especially those wanting joint-friendly movement. This class offers:

Full-body conditioning

Improved endurance & cardio health

Low impact on knees, hips, and back

Enhanced mobility

Refreshing, enjoyable workout experience

It’s perfect for residents who want an effective workout without strain — plus, the water adds natural resistance to increase strength.
